## What this project claims, and what it does not

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers **this project's code only**. It does not
extend to the advisory data.

The advisories remain the work of INCOIS. This project reformats them: it converts
coordinates from degrees-minutes-seconds, recomputes bearing and distance from a
chosen home port, and republishes the result as JSON, GPX and text. It does not
alter the underlying advisory, and it does not assert any ownership of it.

Every published file and every screen of the app carries the credit:

> Source: INCOIS, Ministry of Earth Sciences, Govt. of India

Anyone reusing the data downstream should credit INCOIS, go to INCOIS as the
authority on it, and not treat this project's output as an official product. This
project is not affiliated with or endorsed by INCOIS.

## Fitness for use

> **Advisory only. Not for navigation or safety of life at sea.**

A Potential Fishing Zone advisory is a forecast of where fish are likely to be. It
is valid for roughly a day, it moves with the oceanographic fronts it is derived
from, and it is frequently unavailable because cloud cover blocks the satellite. It
is not a chart, not a navigational aid, and no substitute for official weather and
sea-state warnings or for a skipper's own judgement.
